Kayo — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Data We Collect

Kayo collects only the data necessary to operate the service:

  • Content you create within the app
  • TikTok account credentials (access tokens) to post on your behalf

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to provide the Kayo service — generating and posting content to your connected social media accounts. We do not sell your data to third parties.

3. TikTok Data

When you connect your TikTok account, we receive an access token that allows us to post content on your behalf. We do not store your TikTok password. You can revoke access at any time from your TikTok account settings.

4. Data Storage

Your content and preferences are stored locally on your device. No personal data is stored on external servers without your explicit consent.

5. Cookies

Kayo does not use tracking cookies.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to access, modify, or delete your data at any time by contacting us.
